MY LEARNING JOURNEY of DATA ANALYTICS

LEARNING INDEPENDENTLY

Developing this website allowed me to explore more about data analytics and it gave me a better understanding of the importance of data analytics in everyday life and across various industries even relevance to the modern workplace. Sparking an even greater interest in the field.

What I Learned

While developing this website, I learned that data analytics is not only simply working with numbers, it involves collecting, preparing, analysing and interpreting information to visualization. So that useful version can be produced and help us to make a better decision.

I also learned how data analytics is applied across different industries, including education, business and healthcare even in daily life. This helped me understand why data-related skills are becoming increasingly important in the digital workplace. Through this assignments, it deepened my understanding of and interest in data analytics.
