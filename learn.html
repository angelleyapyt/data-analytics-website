THE MEANINGS

WHAT IS DATA ANALYTICS?

Data analytics is the process of collecting, transforming, and organizing data in order to become visualization to make predictions, and drive informed decision-making.

Organisations use data analytics to transform raw data into meaningful insights. These insights can help employees make informed decisions, improve performance, understand customers'needs and identify potential problems.

FOUR TYPES

THE FOUR KEY TYPES OF DATA ANALYTICS

01

Descriptive Analytics

Telling us "What happened?".

The process of using current and historical data to identify trends and relationship. It's useful for drive decision-making.

Example: Analysing monthly sales figures.

02

Diagnostic Analytics

Tell us "Why did it happen?".

The process of using data to determine the causes of trends and correlations between variables.

Example: Finding out why sales decreased or increase.

03

Predictive Analytics

Tell us "What might happen in the future?"

Predictive analytics uses historical data, statistics and models to predict future trends.

Example: Predicting future customer demand.

04

Prescriptive Analytics

Tell us "What should we do next?"

The process of using data to determine an optimal course of action for recommendations for next steps.

Example: Recommending a strategy to improve sales.

WHY DOES IT MATTER?

ADVANTAGES OF DATA ANALYTICS

📈 Better Decisions-Making

Replaces guesswork with data-driven insights to make accurate, strategic choices based on trends and patterns.

⚡ Improved Operational Efficiency

Identifies bottlenecks, waste, and slow processes, allowing companies to streamline workflows and reduce costs.

👥 Deeper Customer Understanding

Could tracks consumer behavior and preferences to deliver personalized experiences, targeted marketing and customized products.

💰 Cost Reduction

Data can help organisations identify unnecessary spending and improve resource allocation.

🔎 Identify Trends

Analytics can reveal patterns and trends that may not be obvious from raw data.

🛡️ Better Risk Management

Organisations can use data to identify potential risks and support preventative strategies.

THINGS TO CONSIDER

LIMITATIONS & CHALLENGES

  • Poor data quality: Inaccurate or incomplete data can lead to incorrect insights and poor decision-making.
  • Data security and privacy risk: Poor security practices not only undermine the trust of customers and stakeholders but can also expose enterprise to legal and financial consequences.
  • Cybersecurity: Data systems may become targets for cyber threats.
  • Cost: Advanced analytics systems and skilled professionals may require significant investment.
  • Skills shortage: The high demand for qualified data scientists and analyst far outpaces the available workforce. This skills gap leaves technical jobs open for weeks or months.
  • Misinterpretation: Incorrect analysis can lead to wrong decisions.
