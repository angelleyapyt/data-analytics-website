REAL-LIFE APPLICATIONS

DATA ANALYTICS IN ACTION

Data analytics has become an important technology across many sectors of modern society.

01 / EDUCATION

Smarter Learning

Educational institutions and teachers can use data analytics to track performance, predict student needs, and improve overall learning outcomes.

  • Tracking student's performance
  • Monitor attendance
  • Personalizing learning
  • Resource planning and administration
Example:

A university can analyse examination results and attendance records to identify students who may benefit from additional academic support and evaluate the effectiveness of academic programs.

02 / BUSINESS

Smarter Business Decisions

Businesses collect information about customers, sales, products and marketing campaigns. Data analytics helps turn this information into useful business insights helps to improve decision-making, optimize operations and increase revenue.

  • Analyse sales performance
  • Understand customer behaviour
  • Forecast product demand
  • Evaluate marketing campaigns
Example:

Online retailers can analyse consumers' purchasing behaviour to identify popular products and improve its marketing strategies.

03 / HEALTHCARE

Better Healthcare Insights

Healthcare organisations can use data analytics to transforms complex medical and operational data into actionable versions to improve patient outcomes, optimize workflows and lower costs.

  • Analyse patient information
  • Monitor healthcare demand
  • Support medical research
  • Improve resource management
Example:

Hospitals can analyse patient and service data to understand demand and improve resource allocation.

04 / DAILY LIFE

Data in Everyday Life

Data analytics is not limited to organisations. Data analytics also used to personalise and improve everyday experiences such as plan travel routes, manage personal finances and receive personalized recommendations.

  • Streaming recommendations
  • Online shopping recommendations
  • Fitness applications and wearable devices
  • Navigation and traffic analysis
Example:

Media social can analyse viewing behaviour to recommend content that may interest users.

WHY IS DATA ANALYTICS IMPORTANT?

Across education, business, healthcare and everyday life, data analytics helps transform information into clear facts. These could support planning, improve efficiency and encourage evidence-based decision making.
