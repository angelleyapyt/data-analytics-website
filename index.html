What is Data Analytics?

Data analytics is the systematic process of examining raw data to uncover hidden patterns, draw insights, and support better decision making.

In the modern workplace, organisations need to collect large amounts of information every day. Data analytics helps organisations understand this information and support better decisions that improve efficiency and revenue.

Data analytics has become an important part of digital workplace technology. From businesses, analysing customer behaviour to spot customer base. From healthcare organisations, studying patient information and improves patient outcomes.
